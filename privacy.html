Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Last Frame handles information in connection with its TikTok integration.

1 Privacy Policy

Last Frame is an independent serialized vertical-drama studio. This Privacy Policy applies to the public integration website and the Last Frame desktop workflow that authorized creators may use to access TikTok-supported developer features.

2 Information We May Receive

Depending on the TikTok permissions an authorized user grants, the integration may receive basic TikTok account or profile information, authorization information, access and refresh tokens, media upload metadata, and technical request or log information needed for operation and security.

3 TikTok Integration

The integration connects an authorized Last Frame creator account to TikTok-supported developer tools. Permission is requested through TikTok's authorization process. GitHub Pages is used only for public application information, policies, and verification materials. It is not the OAuth token backend.

4 How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate the authorized TikTok account, enable requested TikTok features, transfer creator-authorized content, maintain security, prevent misuse, and diagnose technical problems. Last Frame does not sell personal information obtained through the TikTok integration.

5 Authentication and Tokens

Access and refresh tokens may be stored when needed to maintain an authorized connection. The desktop application is designed to keep sensitive credentials in protected local storage rather than public website files. Tokens are used only for authorized integration functions and may be revoked through TikTok or by disconnecting the integration.

6 Content and Upload Information

The integration may process completed media selected by an authorized creator, along with titles, captions, privacy settings, upload status, and related technical metadata. Content is transferred only when the creator requests the relevant action.

7 Technical and Security Information

Operational records may include timestamps, request status, error details, application version, and device or network information reasonably necessary to secure and troubleshoot the service. Logs should not contain secrets unless technically necessary, and sensitive values are protected or redacted where practicable.

8 Data Sharing

Information may be shared with TikTok when needed to provide requested TikTok features, with service providers that support secure operation, or when required by law. Last Frame does not share TikTok integration information for unrelated advertising or data-broker activity.

9 Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the integration, maintain security, satisfy creator requests, resolve technical issues, and meet applicable obligations. Retention periods may vary by information type. Tokens may remain until they expire, are revoked, or are no longer needed.

10 Data Security

Last Frame uses reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect integration information. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should protect their TikTok account credentials and report suspected unauthorized access.

11 User Choices

Authorized users may decline requested permissions, revoke TikTok access through their TikTok settings where available, disconnect the integration, or contact Last Frame about access or deletion questions. Some features will not function without the permissions needed to provide them.

12 Third-Party Services

The integration relies on third-party services, including TikTok and GitHub Pages. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Last Frame does not control the availability or independent practices of those services.

13 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the integration, applicable requirements, or operational practices change. The current version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
