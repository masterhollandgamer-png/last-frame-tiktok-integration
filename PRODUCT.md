# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

TikTok developer reviewers, authorized Last Frame creators, platform administrators, and members of the public who need to understand the application, its policies, and its operating boundaries.

## Product Purpose

This static site publicly identifies Last Frame and documents the privacy and service terms for its TikTok integration. It gives TikTok a stable website, policy URLs, and verification-file host while keeping desktop OAuth credentials and token exchange outside GitHub Pages.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, restrained, credible. The site should feel like a composed studio title sequence translated into a public application record: confident without spectacle, warm without sentimentality, and precise enough for developer review.

## Anti-references

Generic AI landing-page templates, cheesy gradients, glassmorphism, streaming-service imitation, unearned TikTok partnership language, decorative motion, fake social proof, fabricated legal identity, and any design that hides policy content behind marketing.

## Design Principles

1. Make legitimacy legible within the first screen.
2. Treat privacy and terms as first-class public documents.
3. Separate the public website from private desktop authentication architecture.
4. Use restraint as evidence of control, not as an excuse for thin content.
5. Keep every claim factual, specific, and easy to verify.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction behavior. Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, readable line lengths, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and plain English throughout.
